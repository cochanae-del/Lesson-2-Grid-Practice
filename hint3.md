At this point hopefully we have our grid setup with 4 columns across, but our navigation isn't in the right place. 

Remember we can use some CSS on individual grid items, like `nav`, to place them in a specific location of our grid.

There's more than one option for the "right" syntax here, but we might recommend using `grid-column` and `grid-row` to do this.

The good news is: after you place the navigation, the rest of the posts just fall into place! No additional CSS required on the article grid items.
